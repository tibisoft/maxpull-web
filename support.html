Support

Need a hand?
We're listening.

Found a bug, have a question, or want to suggest something for MaxPull? Send us an email and include enough detail for us to reproduce the issue.

Contact MaxPull support

Email is the simplest way to reach us. If you're reporting a bug, it helps to include your device model, iOS version, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead.

support@tibisoft.nl

Does MaxPull require an account?

No. The landing page describes MaxPull as account-free, with workout data staying on your device.

How do I report a bug?

Email support with a short description of the problem and the steps that led to it. Screenshots are welcome when they help explain what you saw.

Can I request a feature?

Absolutely. Tell us what you would like MaxPull to do and, ideally, why it would make your workouts better.

What information should I include?

For technical issues, include your device model, iOS version, app version if available, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Please don't send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.
