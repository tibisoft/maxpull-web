Privacy

Your workout data
stays yours.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

MaxPull is designed to work without an account, subscription, or cloud profile. Your working max, level progress, workout history, and settings are stored locally on your device rather than on a MaxPull server. The app is supported by ads, which is the one place third-party data collection comes in — details below.

What MaxPull stores on your device

MaxPull uses information on your device to provide the app's core features, such as your working max, level progress, workout history, settings, and streaks. This information is stored locally by the app and is not uploaded to a MaxPull account or server — there isn't one.

Advertising

MaxPull shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the app free with no subscription or purchase required. To do this, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use device identifiers, approximate location, and other technical information to serve and measure ads, under their own privacy policies.

Before showing personalized ads, MaxPull uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent (required for users in the UK/EEA) and Apple's App Tracking Transparency to ask permission to use the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. You can decline either prompt and still use every core feature of the app. You can change your ad consent choice at any time from Settings → Manage Ad Consent, and control app tracking permissions from your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

See Google's how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services for more detail on their data practices.

What MaxPull does not require

  • No account or login.
  • No subscription or in-app purchase — every core feature is free.
  • No MaxPull server account containing your workout history.
  • No sharing of your workout history itself with advertisers — only the ad-technical identifiers described above are involved, not your reps, sets, or progress.

Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your request. We use that information to respond to you and provide support.

Website privacy

This website is a static site and does not provide a MaxPull login or workout-data service. The site currently loads its typography from Google Fonts, so visiting a page that uses those fonts may cause your browser to request font resources from Google.

The site is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with serving the website under its own policies.

Changes to this policy

If the way MaxPull handles information changes, this page will be updated to describe the new practice. The "Last updated" date above indicates when this page was most recently revised.
